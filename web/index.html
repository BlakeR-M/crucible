One run of Crucible raised 16 findings and kept 9.

The other seven met three verifiers whose instruction was to disprove them, and a verifier that stays unsure counts the same as one that disproved it. Two of three decide, so a finding reaches you on agreement rather than on a hunch.

What survives comes with steps you can run. That recorded run cost 27 US cents, and its full log sits in the repository where one command replays every decision in it on your machine.

A written policy gates every agent action before it runs. Every run is written to a hash chain, and your browser checks that chain here without asking this server to vouch for it.
